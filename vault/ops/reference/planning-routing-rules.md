# Planning Routing Rules

> These govern items **not** on your journal horizon lists. An item you've placed under a heading takes its horizon from that heading. `[LOCK]` overrides everything.

**Four layers:** journal horizon lists = what you chose and when · weekly plan = this week's active work · daily plan = weekly plan + today's incomplete items (re-tiered) · `backlog.md` = long-tail graveyard. `commitments.md` is separate — promises to others with dates.

1. **Weekly plan absorbs prior-week carry-forwards.** Read the prior week's summary and open items from last week's daily summaries. Tier in context — theme, Big 5 leverage, calendar capacity, real urgency — never auto-Tier 1 because it's old. Unselected items → `backlog.md` tagged `from: YYYY-WXX`, or kill (logged in the summary). With horizon lists in use, carry-forwards not on your lists go to **Suggested Adds**.
2. **Daily plan reads the weekly plan only** — plus journal Today/This Week lists, today's incomplete items and the calendar. Not prior summaries, prior daily plans or prior weekly plans. The weekly plan is the integration point.
3. **Within-week carry-forwards are re-tiered, not auto-promoted.** Today's incomplete items compete fresh with tomorrow's weekly-plan items.
4. **Quick captures (phone reminders, sticky notes) default to Tier 3 or backlog.** Tier 1/2 only with a firm deadline, a dependency or real leverage. When in doubt, ask.
5. **`backlog.md` is the graveyard, not the conveyor belt.** 2+ weeks unselected → backlog. Reviewed at week-close. 60+ days unselected → `[AI-KILL <date>: 60+ days unselected]`.
6. **End-of-day captures; it doesn't pre-tier.** Tiering happens when the next plan is built.
7. **Type-tag every weekly item.** ⚡ Quick Hit (≤30 min, survives interruption) → slot into work-week gaps. 🧠 Deep Work (needs an uninterrupted block) → Deep Work Docket first, with a named block and any prerequisites (childcare, materials) confirmed. Unslotted 3+ weeks → commit or kill. If the type isn't obvious, ask.

**Stall Scan (weekly close):** any item on its 2nd+ week of carry gets a forced **4R** decision — re-scope, re-slot, re-assign or retire. No item rides 3 weeks unchanged.

**Anti-patterns:** auto-promoting slipped items to Tier 1 · re-sorting the user's lists into the horizons you think they belong in · running a higher-level plan before the week-close cascade has refreshed its inputs.
